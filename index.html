I'm an undergraduate studying Intelligent Equipment Engineering at China Agricultural University. I'm also a research intern at Tsinghua University's LEAP Lab, working with Prof. Gao Huang.

My research interests lie in robot learning, with a focus on embodied agents and harnesses. I'm interested in how general-purpose models and vision-language-action (VLA) policies can support robotic behavior, and how to evaluate these systems across tasks and conditions.

I'm passionate about understanding how robots learn and am actively seeking direct-entry PhD opportunities.

Research interests

Looking ahead

Robot benchmarking & evaluation

I want to understand the strengths and limits of different approaches to robot intelligence. I'm interested in benchmarks that help us compare embodied agents, VLA policies, and their combinations across tasks and conditions.

Harnesses for embodied agents

I'm interested in how harnesses connect general-purpose models with perception, planning, and action. I explore how these systems can work with learned robot policies to carry out tasks in the physical world.
